Pecunity

Community Growth & Campaign Execution Case Study

Joined:Q2 2025
Duration:9 Months

01 — Overview

Project Background

Pecunity is a decentralized finance protocol focused on automated, cross-chain passive income strategies. I joined during its early stage when it was still operating as OctoDeFi and contributed through its rebranding and growth phase.

02 — Challenge

The Problem

Low Discord engagement across channels
Limited campaign participation from the community
Weak X (Twitter) visibility and outreach
Operational gaps in campaign coordination
Early-stage structural challenges during rebranding
03 — Action

What I Did

Onboarded DeFi-focused community members strategically
Amplified Zealy campaign participation through targeted outreach
Improved Discord activity flow and channel structure
Coordinated end-to-end campaign execution
Supported rebranding transition from OctoDeFi to Pecunity
Increased visibility through structured X engagement and threading
04 — Results

Outcomes Delivered

📈Increased campaign participation rate across Zealy and Discord
💬Higher Discord activity and improved member retention
🔍Improved X visibility during campaigns and launch periods
🤝Direct founder acknowledgment and public testimonial received
