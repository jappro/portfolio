Pecunity

Community Growth & Campaign Execution Case Study

Joined Q2 2025
Duration 9 Months
Role Community Manager · Campaign Lead · Growth Strategist
01

Overview

Pecunity is a decentralized finance protocol focused on automated, cross-chain passive income strategies. I joined during its early stage when it was still operating as OctoDeFi and contributed through its rebranding and growth phase.

My work spanned community management, campaign coordination, and growth strategy — helping the project establish a credible, engaged community at a critical stage of development.

02

Challenge

  • Low Discord engagement and inactive member base
  • Limited campaign participation across platforms
  • Weak X (Twitter) visibility during key launch windows
  • Operational gaps in campaign coordination
  • Early-stage structural challenges during rebranding
03

Action

  • Onboarded DeFi-focused community members with targeted outreach
  • Amplified Zealy campaign participation through structured task design
  • Improved Discord activity flow and moderation consistency
  • Coordinated campaign execution across multiple platforms
  • Supported the OctoDeFi → Pecunity rebranding transition
  • Increased visibility through structured X engagement strategies
04

Results

Increased campaign participation rate across Zealy and social channels

Higher Discord activity and improved member retention

Improved X visibility during campaign windows

Direct founder acknowledgment and public testimonial received

"@Japheth did a great job. I highly recommend him for his passionate and modest community work."
Lars — Co-Founder, Pecunity
