Community Growth & Campaign Execution Case Study

JoinedQ2 2025
Duration9 Months
RoleCommunity Manager · Campaign Lead · Growth Strategist
01

Overview

Pecunity is a decentralized finance protocol focused on automated, cross-chain passive income strategies. I joined during its early stage when it was still operating as OctoDeFi and contributed through its rebranding and growth phase.

My work spanned community management, campaign coordination, and growth strategy — helping the project establish a credible, engaged community at a critical stage of development.

02

Challenge

  • Low Discord engagement and an inactive member base with little daily activity
  • Limited campaign participation across Zealy and social platforms
  • Weak X (Twitter) visibility during key presale and launch windows
  • Operational gaps in campaign coordination and task management
  • Early-stage structural challenges compounded by a full rebranding transition
03

Action

  • Onboarded DeFi-focused community members through targeted outreach and onboarding flows
  • Amplified Zealy campaign participation by designing structured, rewarding task frameworks
  • Improved Discord activity and created a consistent moderation cadence
  • Coordinated campaign execution across Discord, X, and Zealy simultaneously
  • Supported the full OctoDeFi → Pecunity rebranding transition with community messaging
  • Drove X visibility through structured engagement strategies during campaign peaks
04

Results

Increased campaign participation rate across Zealy and social channels

Higher Discord activity and measurably improved member retention

Improved X visibility and reach during campaign windows

Direct founder acknowledgment and a public testimonial from Co-Founder Lars

@Japheth did a great job. I highly recommend him for his passionate and modest community work.
Lars — Co-Founder, Pecunity
