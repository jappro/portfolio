01

Overview

Vexor Network is a gaming-driven Web3 community focused on engagement-driven ecosystem participation. I joined as an Ambassador, contributing through daily community support, game night organisation, and active campaign participation.

My role demanded both visibility and reliability — showing up consistently enough to rank in the top tier of the ambassador leaderboard while genuinely helping grow the community from the inside out.

02

Challenge

  • Community needed sustained, long-term engagement — not one-off activity bursts
  • Ambassador programme required consistent, measurable daily participation to rank and maintain standing
  • Gaming communities churn fast without reliable, present community leads to host events and maintain energy
03

Action

  • Organised and hosted weekly community game nights that increased live participation and strengthened member retention month over month
  • Provided real-time community support daily, helping convert inbound interest into active, participating members
  • Reactivated dormant members through targeted engagement initiatives and consistent community interaction
  • Contributed to 30% sustained community activity during campaign periods and ecosystem events
  • Guided new members through onboarding and established community norms from day one, reducing early drop-off
