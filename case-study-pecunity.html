Community Growth & Campaign Execution Case Study

Joined Q2 2025
Duration 9 Months
Role Community Manager · Campaign Lead · Growth Strategist
01

Overview

Pecunity is a decentralized finance protocol focused on automated, cross-chain passive income strategies. I joined during its early stage when it was still operating as OctoDeFi and contributed through its rebranding and growth phase.

My work spanned community management, campaign coordination, and growth strategy — helping the project establish a credible, engaged community at a critical stage of development.

02

Challenge

  • Low Discord engagement and an inactive member base with little daily activity
  • Limited campaign participation across Zealy and social platforms
  • Weak X (Twitter) visibility during key presale and launch windows
  • Operational gaps in campaign coordination and task management
  • Early-stage structural challenges compounded by a full rebranding transition from OctoDeFi to Pecunity
03

Action

  • Designed engagement loops that achieved 90% consistent participation across campaign activities
  • Built and executed onboarding flows that converted new users from initial curiosity into active community members
  • Coordinated campaign execution simultaneously across Discord, X, and Zealy — driving structured task participation and cross-platform visibility
  • Drove organic ecosystem awareness through structured conversations and consistent social promotion
  • Supported the full OctoDeFi → Pecunity rebranding transition with community messaging and continuity
  • Sustained engagement momentum throughout campaign cycles, keeping participation active during growth phases and presale windows
