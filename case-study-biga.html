01

Overview

Biga Arcade is a GameFi-focused community built around competitive and social gaming experiences. As a Biga Master and Community Supporter, I worked to keep the ecosystem active, welcoming, and consistently engaged across both Discord and X.

My role centred on live participation, daily moderation support, and building the consistent event culture that gaming communities depend on to retain members long-term.

02

Challenge

  • Large, fast-moving gaming community requiring continuous and attentive moderation
  • Required structured engagement consistency to prevent member drop-off between events
  • Needed stronger live participation and real-time energy during game nights and streams
03

Action

  • Provided real-time community support across all Discord channels daily
  • Maintained an active, welcoming server environment to reduce member churn
  • Hosted and co-facilitated weekly game nights to build social cohesion
  • Streamed gameplay sessions on YouTube to amplify community visibility
  • Consistently promoted events, highlights, and community updates on X
04

Results

Improved game night participation and consistent attendance week over week

Stronger daily chat engagement and increased member-to-member interaction

Positive recognition and direct testimonial from community leadership

Japheth brought energy and consistency to our community events and streams. Always present, always contributing to the vibe.
Mr Konsole — Admin, Biga Arcade
